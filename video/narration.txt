No car, but still paying for parking? Take a two-bedroom flat in Pyrmont at about $1,010 a week. How much of that pays for empty concrete?
Our model prices both, for every flat. About $935 without the space, so the space costs about $75 a week, or $3,900 a year.
A tenant with no car can ask for the flat without it. An owner can lease the space to someone who needs it.
Tested on 2,635 later adverts it never saw, our typical miss is about $40 a week, and 81% land within $100.
And parking isn't worth the same everywhere: about $70 in Pyrmont, about $26 in Waterloo. Value follows scarcity.
Know what you are paying for. 2 Bulbs 1 Source.
